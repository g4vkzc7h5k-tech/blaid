Refund Policy

Monthly Subscriptions

You can cancel anytime from your Discord Settings → Subscriptions → Premium App Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future billing, but the current paid period stays active until it ends - no partial refund for time already paid for. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us within 7 days of the charge.

Lifetime (One-Time) Purchases

Lifetime purchases are final and non-refundable once premium has been activated on your server, per Discord's own Premium Apps policy. If the purchase failed to activate premium within 24 hours, contact us and we'll look into it.

How Billing Works

All payments are processed entirely by Discord - we never see or store your card details. Manage payment methods, view receipts, and cancel subscriptions directly from your Discord User Settings → Billing.

Contact

For billing issues, refund requests, or questions, reach out in our support server or DM the bot owner directly.
