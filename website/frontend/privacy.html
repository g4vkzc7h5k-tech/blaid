Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This is a starting template, not legal advice. It's written to genuinely reflect what Blade actually stores based on its current features, but you (the operator) are responsible for having it reviewed by a lawyer for your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR/CCPA obligations) and for filling in the bracketed placeholders below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data Blade ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects when it's added to a Discord server, how it's used, and how you can request it be removed.

2. Data We Collect

Depending on which features a server enables, the Bot may store:

  • Identifiers: Discord server, channel, role, and user IDs - needed to know which server a setting applies to and who a command targets.
  • Moderation records: ban/kick/warn/timeout case history, jail records, and chat-filter strike counts, tied to a server and user ID.
  • Server configuration: settings server admins choose - filtered words/phrases/regex patterns, antinuke module settings and admin lists, fake permissions granted to roles, level roles and XP multipliers, giveaway settings, ticket panel/option configuration, welcome/leave/boost message templates, and similar.
  • Leveling data: per-user XP and level, per server.
  • Ticket transcripts: a plaintext log of messages sent in a ticket channel, saved when the ticket is closed.
  • Message content, transiently: the chat filter and autoresponder features read message content to check it against configured rules at the moment a message is sent. Message content itself is not stored, except where it becomes part of a ticket transcript as described above.

We do not collect payment information, and the Bot does not have access to direct messages outside of ticket-related notifications it sends you.

3. How We Use Data

Data is used solely to provide the Bot's features in the server(s) it's added to - for example, showing your level, honoring a ticket's configuration, or enforcing a configured punishment. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose unrelated to operating the Bot.

4. Data Retention

Server-specific data is retained for as long as the Bot remains in that server. Removing the Bot from a server does not necessarily delete that server's data immediately, but you can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

5. Third Parties

The Bot operates through Discord's API and is hosted on [Your Hosting Provider]. These third parties process data as necessary to run the Bot, under their own respective privacy policies. We do not share data with any other third party.

6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is only used through Discord, which has its own minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data beyond what a server's use of Discord and the Bot already involves, and do not knowingly target the Bot at anyone below Discord's minimum age.

7. Your Rights

You can request a copy of, or deletion of, data associated with your Discord user ID or your server, by reaching out through our support server. We will respond and act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or data requests, can be sent through our support server.
