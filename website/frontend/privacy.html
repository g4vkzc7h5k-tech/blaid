Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what data blaid ("we", "us", "the bot") collects when you use blaid on Discord or visit blaid.best, why we collect it, and how you can request it be removed.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect what's needed to make blaid's features work. This includes:

  • Discord IDs — your user ID, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs, wherever a feature needs to remember them (e.g. a configured log channel, a moderation case, a leveling rank).
  • Command usage data — the content of commands you run, only where a feature requires storing it (e.g. a custom autoresponder trigger, a saved embed, a ticket panel's settings).
  • Moderation records — bans, kicks, warns, timeouts, and jail actions are logged with the moderator's ID, the target's ID, and the reason given, so server staff can review history via commands like ,history.
  • Website account data — if you log in with Discord on blaid.best (e.g. to build a ticket panel), we temporarily store your Discord user ID, username, and avatar hash for the duration of your session.

We do not read, log, or store the content of your regular messages. Features like ,snipe hold a small number of recently deleted/edited messages in memory only, and that data is lost the moment the bot restarts.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the features you and your server configure (moderation, leveling, tickets, autoresponders, and so on).
  • To enforce server-specific settings like blacklists, permission overrides, and premium status.
  • To respond to bug reports submitted via ,bug.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Payments

Premium purchases are processed entirely through Discord's own App Monetization system. We never see, collect, or store your payment card, bank, or billing details — Discord handles that directly. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made (which plan, and for which server), so we can activate it.

4. Data Retention

Configuration and moderation data is kept for as long as blaid is in your server, so history and settings remain available. If blaid is removed from a server, that server's stored settings are deleted automatically within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Reach out in our support server or DM the bot owner directly. Server administrators can also remove most stored data themselves via the bot's own reset/clear commands (e.g. ,warn clear, ,invoke reset).

6. Third-Party Services

Some features call external APIs to work (for example, image or GIF sources, translation, or AI answers). These requests may include the text or image you provided to that command, sent only to fulfill that specific request - not stored by us beyond what's needed to reply.

7. Children's Privacy

blaid is intended for use in accordance with Discord's own Terms of Service, which requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect additional data from children beyond what Discord itself provides to any bot in a server.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as blaid's features change. Significant changes will be announced in our support server. Continuing to use blaid after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out in our support server.
